Methodology

This report analyzes 87,672 hourly weather observations from 2015-01-01 to 2024-12-31 at coordinates 16.725°N, 88.84°W (Belize). Data sourced from the Open-Meteo Archive API (ERA5 reanalysis). Scoring thresholds match the FarmWeather live app: Humidity (>40% safe, 30–40% caution, <30% danger), Wind (<5 mph safe, 5–10 caution, >10 danger), Temperature (<80°F safe, 80–90° caution, >90° danger). Overall status is the worst of the three metrics.
